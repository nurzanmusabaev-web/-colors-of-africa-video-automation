What if everything you think you know about Africa is wrong?

It is not one country. It is 54 of them, over 2,000 languages, and a continent so big you could fit the United States, China, India and most of Europe inside its borders with room to spare. Sudan alone has twice as many pyramids as Egypt — older, steeper, and almost unknown outside Africa. This film runs through nine facts about the continent that get left out of the version most people learn.

🌍 9 FACTS IN THIS FILM
• Africa covers over 30 million km² — the second-largest landmass on Earth
• Sudan has 223 pyramids at Meroe, built by the Kingdom of Kush — more than double Egypt's count, and older
• Wild African penguins breed on the South African coast; locals call them "jackass penguins" for the donkey-like bray
• Over 2,000 languages are spoken in Africa — more than a quarter of all languages on Earth; Nigeria alone has over 500
• Elephants dig wells in dry riverbeds during drought, smelling water from up to 12 miles away — the whole ecosystem drinks after they do
• Giraffes sleep roughly 30 minutes a day, resting standing up to stay safe from lions
• Every human lineage traces back to Africa, where it first appeared over 4 million years ago
• Africa holds roughly 90% of the world's platinum, 40% of its gold, and 60% of its cobalt
• 1.5 million wildebeest migrate 500 miles a year across Tanzania and Kenya, through crocodile-filled rivers
