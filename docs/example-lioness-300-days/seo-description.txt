Lioness: 300 Days From Blind Cub to Her First Kill

She was born blind, deaf and helpless in a thorn thicket — one of four. Three hundred days later only she is still alive, and she is standing over her own kill. This is that story, told day by day.

Lion cubs are born with their eyes sealed shut and weigh barely 1.5 kg. They cannot see for over a week, cannot walk for two, and cannot hunt for the better part of a year. In the wild, only about one cub in eight reaches adulthood. This wildlife documentary follows a single lioness across the 300 days that decide whether she becomes one of them.

No narration. No music telling you how to feel. Just the days, in order.

📊 FACTS IN THIS FILM
• Newborn lion cubs weigh 1.2-1.6 kg and are born with their eyes closed
• Eyes open between day 3 and day 11; the blue-grey iris turns amber by month 3
• Cubs are hidden alone in a den for the first 6-8 weeks
• Milk teeth break through at around 3 weeks, first meat at 3 months
• Fully weaned at 8-10 months, but real hunting skill takes 2 years
• Roughly 1 in 8 wild lion cubs survives to adulthood
