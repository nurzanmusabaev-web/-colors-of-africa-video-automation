🐧 FACTS IN THIS FILM
- African penguins are the only penguin species that breeds in Africa — colonies at Boulders Beach and Robben Island, South Africa, and along the Namibian coast
- Nicknamed the "jackass penguin" for its loud, donkey-like bray
- In 2024 the IUCN reclassified the African penguin as Critically Endangered — the first of 18 penguin species worldwide to reach that status
- The population has fallen roughly 97% in a century, from around 1 million breeding pairs to only a few thousand today
- Chicks fledge into sleek blue-grey juvenile plumage at 2-4 months old, then swim out to sea completely on their own — no parent follows
- Threats include kelp gulls and mongooses on land, oil spills, and competition with commercial fishing fleets for the sardines and anchovies the colony depends on
